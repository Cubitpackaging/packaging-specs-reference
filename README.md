## Quick Reference: Corrugated Flute Thickness & Typical Uses

| Flute | Approx. Thickness | Typical ECT Grades | Common Applications |
|---|---|---|---|
| **F-Flute** | ~0.8 mm (1/32") | 26–32 ECT | Compact retail cartons, cosmetics, lightweight DTC mailers |
| **E-Flute** | ~1.5 mm (1/16") | 32 ECT | Subscription & DTC mailer boxes, crisp litho/digital printing |
| **B-Flute** | ~3.0 mm (1/8") | 32–44 ECT | Heavier retail mailers, inner shipping boxes, puncture resistance |
| **C-Flute** | ~4.0 mm (5/32") | 32–44 ECT | Standard RSC shipping cartons and transit master cases |
| **BC Double-Wall** | ~7.0 mm (9/32") | 44–51 ECT | Heavy bulk shipments, fragile glass, palletized export cartons |
